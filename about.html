Tibetan Mastiff preservation

Preserving the legacy of the Tibetan Mastiff.

Founded over 20 years ago, Kyra Kennels began with a deep passion for preserving this ancient guardian and a vision from our late father, Col. Kuldeep Singh.

A vision carried forward

Over the last decade, we have witnessed the Tibetan Mastiff face a downward trajectory due to increasing market demand, careless breeding practices, and dilution of original bloodlines. The majestic guardian of the Tibetan Plateau began losing its true essence.

Our mission remains clear: to restore this magnificent breed to its former glory.

We carefully handpick our lines directly from the Tibetan Plateau and the historic Hequ region, working with dedicated breeders who share our commitment to authenticity. Our gene pool includes recognized Panda, Jin Tai, and traditional Tibetan lines.

Beyond names and recognition, we focus on safeguarding some of the oldest and purest bloodlines that still exist.

Beyond pedigree names

A thoughtful breeding philosophy

Our decisions center on the qualities that preserve the true breed type and working heritage:

  • Health
  • Correct structure
  • Sound temperament
  • Functional movement
  • True breed type

We believe exceptional guardians carry strength, dignity, and sound character into the generations ahead.
