Our Caucasian Shepherds

A powerful guardian with noble character.

Our breeding program honours the heritage, strength, and natural guarding instincts of the Caucasian Shepherd.

Bloodlines from Russia and Romania

Our Caucasian Shepherds are bred with a deep appreciation for the breed's rich heritage as one of the world's most formidable livestock and property guardians. Our program incorporates carefully selected bloodlines from Russia and Romania, combining genetics that exemplify the strength, character, and presence for which the breed is renowned.

Among the distinguished bloodlines represented in our kennel are descendants of the legendary Brailita Caucasians, respected for their size, substance, powerful heads, and exceptional breed type.

Strength, structure, and balance

Our goal is to produce dogs with the impressive bone, substantial size, and commanding presence expected of the breed, while maintaining sound structure, balanced movement, and stable temperament. True quality lies not only in appearance but also in health, functionality, and the natural instincts that have defined the Caucasian Shepherd for centuries.

A devoted family guardian

The remarkable size, powerful head structure, and imposing physique of our dogs appeal to enthusiasts who appreciate giant guardian breeds. Beyond their appearance lies a loyal, intelligent, and devoted companion capable of forming strong family bonds while retaining the confidence and courage that are hallmarks of the breed.

Kyra Kennels remains committed to preserving the authenticity, health, and working heritage of the Caucasian Shepherd, ensuring each generation reflects the strength, character, and nobility that make this breed a true guardian legend.
