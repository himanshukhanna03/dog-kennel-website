Our Tibetan Mastiffs

Preserving an ancient guardian.

At Kyra Kennels, our Tibetan Mastiffs represent decades of careful research, selective breeding, and an unwavering commitment to preserving the true essence of this ancient breed.

Bloodlines, heritage, and purpose

Our breeding program combines exceptional dogs from established European programs and native Tibetan bloodlines to maintain genetic diversity, health, and authentic breed characteristics.

Among the foundations of our program are descendants of the legendary Hequ Tibetan Mastiff known as Panda, as well as bloodlines from the highly respected Jin Tai Kennel. These pedigrees are incorporated not simply for prestige, but to strengthen the genetic pool and preserve structural integrity, working ability, and traditional character.

In recent years, the breed has undergone significant changes due to indiscriminate breeding, exaggerated physical traits, and marketing-driven trends. Commercial appeal, sensational claims, and misunderstood concepts such as DNA certification can create confusion among prospective owners.

Preserving the Tibetan Mastiff requires a deeper understanding of the breed's history, purpose, and development. We focus on dogs true to type: powerful yet balanced in structure, sound in temperament, equipped with natural guarding instincts, and dignified in presence.

A long-term commitment

Every breeding decision is made with long-term preservation in mind. We prioritize health, temperament, longevity, and functional conformation above temporary trends or exaggerated features.

Our goal is not simply to breed Tibetan Mastiffs, but to safeguard the future of the breed by producing dogs faithful to their heritage and able to thrive in the modern world.

At Kyra Kennels, authenticity is not a marketing slogan. It is the foundation of everything we do.

Our Tibetan Mastiffs

Meet Kyra Dorjee and Kyra Bale.

From Jin Tai Kennel

Kyra Dorjee

Dorjee is a one-of-a-kind male from the renowned Jin Tai Kennel. Large and impressive, he carries tremendous substance, bone, and presence.

His rich sable coat is a crowd favourite. A beautiful, well-defined head complements his powerful structure and overall balance. His offspring are already making a strong mark in India, a testament to his quality and prepotency.

We are proud of this stud and his role in helping preserve and promote the true essence of the Tibetan Mastiff.

From a Hequ conservation centre

Kyra Bale

Kyra Bale comes from China, originating from one of the oldest Hequ Tibetan Mastiff conservation centres. Rooted in authentic bloodlines, Bale represents heritage, strength, and true breed type.

His magnificent lion-like mane makes him a standout. Compact, powerful, and highly athletic, he combines substance with agility. He has helped improve and strengthen the breed in India, consistently passing on type, structure, and presence.

Beyond his commanding appearance, Bale has a loving and stable temperament. We are proud to have him contributing to the preservation of the true Tibetan Mastiff.
