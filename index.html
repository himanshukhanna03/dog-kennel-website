Tibetan Mastiff preservation

Preserving the legacy of the Tibetan Mastiff.

Kyra Kennels was founded over 20 years ago with a deep passion for preserving this ancient breed and a vision from our late father, Col. Kuldeep Singh.

A vision carried forward

Over the last decade, we have witnessed the breed face a downward trajectory due to increasing market demand, careless breeding practices, and dilution of original bloodlines. The majestic guardian of the Tibetan Plateau began losing its true essence.

Our mission remains clear: to restore this magnificent breed to its former glory.

We carefully handpick our lines directly from the Tibetan Plateau and the historic Hequ region, working with dedicated breeders who share our commitment to authenticity. Our gene pool includes Panda, Jin Tai, and traditional Tibetan lines, and we focus on safeguarding some of the oldest and purest bloodlines that still exist.

Research before every pairing

Thoughtful breeding, built on purpose.

Every breeding decision is guided by extensive research, careful planning, and an unwavering commitment to quality.

Bloodlines with purpose

We hand-select exceptional bloodlines from across the globe, sourcing from renowned breeders and proven working pedigrees. This international approach combines genetic diversity with breed purity in a responsible, forward-thinking program.

The true essence of a breed lies not only in appearance, but in temperament, instinct, and character. We work to preserve the natural traits that made these breeds legendary guardians.

Health is the cornerstone

Every breeding pair is carefully evaluated for health, structural soundness, and temperament. We strive to produce physically robust, mentally stable, healthy, confident, and well-balanced companions capable of fulfilling their original roles.

We are not driven by trends or quantity. Each litter represents years of planning and dedication to the highest standards.

Our commitment is to safeguard these breeds with dogs that reflect their strength, intelligence, courage, and character.

Our Tibetan Mastiffs

Preserving an ancient guardian.

Our Tibetan Mastiffs represent decades of careful research, selective breeding, and commitment to preserving the true essence of this ancient breed.

Bloodlines, heritage, and purpose

Our program combines exceptional dogs from established European programs and native Tibetan bloodlines to maintain genetic diversity, health, and authentic breed characteristics.

Among its foundations are descendants of the legendary Hequ Tibetan Mastiff known as Panda and bloodlines from the respected Jin Tai Kennel. These pedigrees strengthen the genetic pool and preserve structural integrity, working ability, and traditional character.

Indiscriminate breeding, exaggerated physical traits, marketing-driven trends, and misunderstood concepts such as DNA certification can create confusion. We focus on dogs true to type: powerful yet balanced in structure, sound in temperament, equipped with natural guarding instincts, and dignified in presence.

Every breeding decision considers health, temperament, longevity, and functional conformation above temporary trends. Our goal is to safeguard the future of the breed while producing dogs faithful to their heritage and able to thrive in the modern world.

Authenticity is not a marketing slogan. It is the foundation of everything we do.

Our Tibetan Mastiffs

Meet Kyra Dorjee and Kyra Bale.

From Jin Tai Kennel

Kyra Dorjee

Dorjee is a one-of-a-kind male from the renowned Jin Tai Kennel. Large and impressive, he carries tremendous substance, bone, and presence.

His rich sable coat is a crowd favourite. A beautiful, well-defined head complements his powerful structure and balance. His offspring are already making a strong mark in India, a testament to his quality and prepotency.

We are proud of this stud and his role in helping preserve and promote the true essence of the Tibetan Mastiff.

From a Hequ conservation centre

Kyra Bale

Kyra Bale comes from China, originating from one of the oldest Hequ Tibetan Mastiff conservation centres. Rooted in authentic bloodlines, Bale represents heritage, strength, and true breed type.

His magnificent lion-like mane makes him a standout. Compact, powerful, and highly athletic, he combines substance with agility. He has helped strengthen the breed in India, consistently passing type, structure, and presence to his offspring.

Beyond his commanding appearance, Bale has a loving and stable temperament. We are proud to have him contributing to the preservation of the true Tibetan Mastiff.

Our South African Boerboels

Strength with sound character.

Our Boerboels are the result of a carefully planned program founded on respected bloodlines in South Africa. We have selectively sourced genetics from breeders and bloodlines such as Afrika, MES, and EBS, aiming to retain the powerful build, correct movement, structural soundness, and balanced temperament of the authentic Boerboel.

Originally developed as a versatile farm guardian, the Boerboel is valued for strength, intelligence, courage, and unwavering family loyalty. Preserving these qualities remains at the heart of our program. Every pairing focuses on breed type, health, functionality, and long-term genetic diversity.

We aim for substantial size without sacrificing athleticism, powerful bone and musculature with efficient movement, and a confident, stable temperament suited to guardianship and family life.

Kyra Kennels is proud to have been the first kennel to introduce the South African Boerboel to India. Through responsible breeding and a commitment to excellence, we continue to contribute to the preservation and advancement of this remarkable breed.

Our Caucasian Shepherds

A powerful guardian with noble character.

Our Caucasian Shepherds are bred with deep appreciation for their heritage as formidable livestock and property guardians. Our program incorporates carefully selected bloodlines from Russia and Romania, combining genetics known for strength, character, and presence.

Among the distinguished lines in our kennel are descendants of the legendary Brailita Caucasians, respected for their size, substance, powerful heads, and exceptional breed type.

Our goal is to produce dogs with impressive bone, substantial size, and commanding presence while maintaining sound structure, balanced movement, and stable temperament. True quality lies in health, functionality, and the natural instincts that have defined the breed for centuries.

Beyond their imposing appearance, these dogs are loyal, intelligent, and devoted companions who form strong family bonds while retaining the confidence and courage that define the breed.

We remain committed to preserving the authenticity, health, and working heritage of the Caucasian Shepherd for generations to come.
