Our South African Boerboels

Strength with sound character.

Our Boerboels are the result of a carefully planned program built around respected South African bloodlines.

Bloodlines selected for quality

We have selectively sourced and incorporated genetics from renowned breeders and bloodlines such as Afrika, MES, and EBS. Our aim is to retain the powerful build, correct movement, structural soundness, and balanced temperament that define the authentic Boerboel.

A versatile farm guardian

The Boerboel was developed as a versatile farm guardian, valued for strength, intelligence, courage, and unwavering loyalty to family. Preserving these qualities remains at the heart of our breeding philosophy.

Every breeding decision focuses on maintaining breed type while promoting health, functionality, and long-term genetic diversity.

Faithful to South African heritage

Our dogs are bred to exhibit the hallmark characteristics of the true South African Boerboel: substantial size without sacrificing athleticism, powerful bone and musculature combined with efficient movement, and a confident, stable temperament that makes them exceptional guardians and family companions.

Kyra Kennels is proud to have been the first kennel to introduce the South African Boerboel to India. From the beginning, our vision has been to establish and promote genuine, high-quality Boerboels faithful to their South African heritage.

Through responsible breeding practices and a commitment to excellence, we continue to contribute to the preservation and advancement of this remarkable breed in India. Our Boerboels reflect our dedication to authenticity, quality, and preservation.
